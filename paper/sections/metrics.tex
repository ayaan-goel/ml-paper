\section{Performance Metrics}\label{sec:metrics}

\subsection{Error Metrics}

For a crop with $N$ test observations, let $y_i$ denote the reported yield of observation $i$ in
t/ha, $\hat{y}_i$ its out-of-fold prediction and $\bar{y}$ the mean of the $y_i$. The root mean
squared error, the mean absolute error and the coefficient of determination are
\begin{align}
\mathrm{RMSE} &= \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left(y_i - \hat{y}_i\right)^2}, \label{eq:rmse}\\
\mathrm{MAE} &= \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left|y_i - \hat{y}_i\right|, \label{eq:mae}\\
R^2 &= 1 - \frac{\sum_{i}\left(y_i - \hat{y}_i\right)^2}{\sum_{i}\left(y_i - \bar{y}\right)^2}, \label{eq:r2}
\end{align}
where $R^2$ compares the model with a predictor that always returns the mean of the evaluated
observations; it can be negative when the model is worse than that predictor. Because the three
crops differ in their yield level, the RMSE is also expressed as a percentage of the mean yield,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{nRMSE} = 100\,\frac{\mathrm{RMSE}}{\bar{y}}, \label{eq:nrmse}
\end{equation}
which allows averages over the crops. All metrics are computed on the pooled out-of-fold
predictions of one seed and then averaged over the three seeds.

\subsection{Derived Metrics}

The \emph{degradation} caused by missing data is the change in nRMSE relative to complete data
under the same model and evaluation design,
\begin{equation}
\Delta\mathrm{nRMSE} = \mathrm{nRMSE}(\rho) - \mathrm{nRMSE}(0), \label{eq:delta}
\end{equation}
in percentage points (pp), where $\rho$ is the missingness rate. To test whether missing data and
geographic shift interact, three effects are measured relative to the random split with complete
data, $\mathrm{nRMSE}_{R,0}$: the geographic effect
$G = \mathrm{nRMSE}_{D,0} - \mathrm{nRMSE}_{R,0}$, the missingness effect
$M = \mathrm{nRMSE}_{R,\rho} - \mathrm{nRMSE}_{R,0}$ and the combined effect
$C = \mathrm{nRMSE}_{D,\rho} - \mathrm{nRMSE}_{R,0}$, where the subscripts $R$ and $D$ denote the
random split and the district holdout. The \emph{interaction} is
\begin{equation}
I = C - (G + M), \label{eq:inter}
\end{equation}
which is positive when the combined loss exceeds the sum of the separate losses, and zero when the
two effects simply add.

\subsection{Statistical Tests}

Uncertainty in an RMSE difference between two conditions evaluated on the same observations was
estimated by a bootstrap over districts \cite{efron1993}: the districts were resampled with
replacement 2,000 times, the RMSE of both conditions was recomputed on the resampled observations,
and the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the difference were reported as a 95\% confidence interval.
Resampling whole districts respects the dependence between observations of the same district.
Following Dem\v{s}ar \cite{demsar2006}, the ten model--strategy combinations were ranked by RMSE
within each crop, pattern and rate under the district holdout, and the ranks were compared with the
Friedman test \cite{friedman1937}. The interaction $I$ in \eqref{eq:inter} was tested against zero
with a one-sample $t$-test over the crop, strategy and pattern combinations.
